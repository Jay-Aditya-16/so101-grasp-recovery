# Phase III hold-verifier development evidence

The local prototype uses wrist-image red-pixel visibility and gripper opening to return held, empty, or unknown. The optional live integration logs decisions in shadow mode only; it does not change robot actions. The offline evaluator compares these cues with privileged simulator contact and object-height proxies. Those proxies are not sensors available on hardware and are not conclusive proof of a stable grasp. This verifier is cube- and scene-specific and remains experimental.

The predeclared `plan.json` has three development and three held-out cases. On the held-out set, all nine evaluated stage labels were held; the recorded variants agreed with all nine. There are no held-out negative labels, so that result does not establish lost-object detection. The low-friction and historical/out-of-distribution analyses provide further development examples, not independent proof of generalization. `matlab-heldout.csv` and `matlab-ood.csv` recount the stored labels; they do not validate the simulator or the reference labels.

High-grasp seed 1060 failed before reaching a grasp; 1061 and 1062 placed successfully. Seed 1063 has no completed result and is not scored. The external-push plan was prepared but never run. See `execution-notes.json` for launchers deliberately terminated after writing completed, validated results. Raw camera and action archives are local under `datasets/local-rollouts/` and are excluded from Git; the JSON summaries and row CSVs here do not substitute for those archives. Further held-out dropped-object and failed-grasp cases are required before control integration.
